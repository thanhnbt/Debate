Day 1 summary

Six ideas to remember

Debate is a structured way to investigate a question, explain a position, answer another team, and make a meaningful comparison.

?

Debate begins with “Why?”

It is not only about being right or wrong. Strong debaters explain their choices and search for the best-supported solution.

◉

Public speaking creates impact

A speech may educate, entertain, or influence. Clear delivery helps an audience understand and remember your case.

↔

Teamwork is essential

A debate case is built together. Speakers share goals, divide responsibilities, support one another, and stay consistent.

⌁

Critical thinking uses evidence

Ask questions, check assumptions, evaluate facts, and remain willing to correct your own reasoning.

3v3

WSDC is a team format

Proposition and Opposition each use three main speakers. Main speeches, reply speeches, and POIs have different purposes.

40

Judges balance three criteria

Content is what you say (40%), style is how you say it (40%), and strategy is why and when you say it (20%).
