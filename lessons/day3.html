What is WSDC?

WSDC stands for World Schools Debating Championship — the set of rules used in the biggest student debate tournament in the world, like FIFA rules for football!

Speaking Turns

Each debate has 8 speaking turns: 6 main speeches (8 minutes each) + 2 Reply speeches (4 minutes each). POI is allowed from minute 1 to minute 7.

PM & LO Roles

The Prime Minister opens the debate with a Case Setup. The Leader of Opposition responds and presents their own arguments.

Core Concept

Building the Foundation for a Debate

Why does the motion exist?

  • → Because we have problems in society
  • → Debate motions are different policies to address those problems
  • → The foundation of a debate requires us to identify the relevant problems and their consequences

Context / Status Quo = the current world that we are living in

Magic Spell
“You have them all when you have context.”
